# <p id="top">TIC TAC TOE GAME - (Computer Vs Human)</p>

## Description 📃
The game features a 3x3 grid where two players alternate placing “X” and “O” marks. Winning is achieved by aligning three marks in a row, column, or diagonal before the opponent. Interactive functions handle user clicks, manage the game state, and determine win or draw outcomes. Players can choose to play against each other or face a simple AI opponent for solo gameplay

## Functionalities 🎮
- Two Game Modes: Play against another human locally or challenge a simple AI that makes random moves.

- Interactive 3x3 Grid: Players click on grid cells to place their marks ("X" or "O"), with turns alternating automatically.

- Win and Draw Detection: The game automatically checks and announces when a player has achieved three in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) or when the board is full and results in a draw.

- Game State Management: The application actively tracks the state of each cell, the current player's turn, and the overall progress of the game.

- Simple and Intuitive UI: Features an easy-to-understand design, allowing users to quickly start and enjoy a game with minimal setup

## How to Play ? 🕹️

- The player with the “X” goes first, followed by the “O” player; players take turns placing their marks in empty spaces.

- The aim is to place three of your marks in a row, whether horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

- The first player to achieve three in a row wins the game.

- If all nine spaces are filled and neither player wins, the game ends in a draw
