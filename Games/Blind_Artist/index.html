📖 How to Play

  1. Click "Start Drawing" to get a random object to draw
  2. Draw on the invisible canvas - you can't see what you're drawing!
  3. You have 60 seconds to complete your drawing
  4. Click "Reveal My Art" when done (or when time runs out)
  5. See your hilarious creation and rate your artwork!
  6. Share your masterpiece with friends! 😄
